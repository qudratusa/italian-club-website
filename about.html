About the Italian Club

The Italian Club is dedicated to promoting Italian culture, language, and traditions among students at UNC Charlotte.

Club details below are preserved from the 2025 academic project and are not verified current club information.

Club history

The original project describes the club as founded in 1975, with a community celebrating Italian heritage through events, activities, and educational opportunities. This historical claim has not been independently verified.
