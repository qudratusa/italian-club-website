About the Italian Club at UNC Charlotte

The Italian Club is dedicated to promoting Italian culture, language, and traditions among students at UNC Charlotte.

Club History

Founded in 1975, the Italian Club has grown to become a vibrant community celebrating Italian heritage through events, activities, and educational opportunities.
